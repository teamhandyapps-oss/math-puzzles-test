How it works

Every topic starts with a worked example that shows each step, then your child tries a few questions with help one tap away. Try it here - this is real practice from the app.

1. A worked example first

8 + 5, the make-ten way

  1. 1Start with 8. We need 2 more to fill a ten-frame.
  2. 2Split 5 into 2 and 3.
  3. 3Make ten: 8 + 2 = 10.
  4. 4Add what is left: 10 + 3.

8 counters2 moved from the 5

So 8 + 5 = 13.
