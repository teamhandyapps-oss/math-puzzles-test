What it means

To add two three-digit numbers, add the ones, then the tens, then the hundreds. When a place adds up to ten or more, exchange ten of that place for one of the next place and carry it over, just like carrying with two-digit numbers.

Worked example: 456 + 278

  1. 1Add the ones: 6 + 8 = 14. Write 4 in the ones place. Carry 1 ten.
  2. 2Add the tens: 5 + 7 = 12, plus the carried ten = 13. Write 3 in the tens place. Carry 1 hundred.
  3. 3Add the hundreds: 4 + 2 = 6, plus the carried hundred = 7.
  4. 47 hundreds, 3 tens and 4 ones.
